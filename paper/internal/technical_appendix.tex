\section{Reproduction and Audit}

The local fault audit and derived analysis require no network call or secret:

\begin{verbatim}
env -u PYTHONPATH conda run -n mlip-research-agent \
  python scripts/research/run_trace_fault_audit.py \
  --output artifacts/reproductions/free-ralph-trace-audit
env -u PYTHONPATH conda run -n mlip-research-agent \
  python scripts/research/build_trace_audit_analysis.py \
  --benchmark artifacts/reproductions/free-ralph-trace-audit \
  --output artifacts/reproductions/free-ralph-trace-analysis
Rscript paper/figures/render_figures.R "$PWD"
\end{verbatim}

The submitted ledger is the original execution ledger. Reproduction creates a new ledger for comparison; it must not overwrite the frozen evidence during audit. Decisions and summary endpoints reproduce, while per-row elapsed timings are host-dependent. Amendment A-002 therefore excludes the under-specified \texttt{wall\_seconds} secondary endpoint from interpretation. The paper was compiled with \texttt{pdflatex -no-shell-escape}; all citations and references resolved, all fonts are embedded, and the three-page letter-size PDF was visually inspected page by page.

\section{Claim Ceiling}

The local fault counts are T1 infrastructure facts, while the reused L4 observations are T2 remote-infrastructure observations. There is no T3--T5 scientific claim. In particular, the evidence does not establish an improved potential, general adversarial robustness, a physically significant rerun delta, or a population false-positive rate of zero.
