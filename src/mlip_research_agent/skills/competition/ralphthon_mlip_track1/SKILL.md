# Ralphthon MLIP Track 1

Use the unmodified official `auto-research` and `vessl-cloud-onboarding`
skills before invoking this typed bridge. Treat those two skills as required
dependencies and this package as the project-specific routing boundary.

## Route

Follow exactly this route:

1. MLIP / Cu / MACE research.
2. Official Ralphthon General Track 1.
3. Project MLIP research controller and independent evaluator.
4. Optional VESSL Cloud compute provider after a sealed live cost approval.
5. Validated 2–4 page Track 1 template and frozen evidence map.
6. Self-review against the same frozen evidence.

Never route MLIP evidence through the Karpathy Training path. Keep the pinned
VESSL cookbook available only for an independent execution of its unchanged
autoresearch benchmark with `val_bpb`. Never combine `val_bpb` with MLIP
energy or force metrics.

## Safety boundary

- Require `scientific_status: infrastructure_only` and `claim_eligible: false`.
- Keep H2, H3, and H5 open; never promote the candidate checkpoint.
- Reject frozen-test data, acquisition-pool labels, stress labels, and full
  protected datasets.
- Keep W&B disabled by default. Offline recording is optional; online sync
  requires a separate allowlist and approval.
- Do not create VESSL Jobs, Workspaces, Volumes, or Storage without an exact,
  sealed cost approval bound to current live inventory and price.

## Output

Produce only a route contract and evidence-binding requirements during local
implementation. Do not draft a claim-bearing paper until a separately
authorized, independently graded run exists.
